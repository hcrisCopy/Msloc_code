Evaluate fluency (Text Coherence & Fluency): the grammar, readability, and logical coherence of the Explanation. Apply the same rubric to every explanation.

5: Smooth, grammatically correct, and logically coherent.
4: Minor grammatical errors or awkward phrasing, fully comprehensible.
3: Some fragmentation or repetition, but the basic meaning is clear.
2: Substantial grammatical or internal logical problems make the explanation difficult to follow.
1: Largely meaningless, incoherent, or unreadable.

This dimension measures language quality independently of factual accuracy.
